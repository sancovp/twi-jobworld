# B6 — copy template + rules (instruction the LLM fills and obeys)

Goal of every send: make a director-level-or-above brand marketer book a 15-minute
call with Mason and Weston. The reader's reaction must be "this is the most
interesting inbound I have ever received."

## The four-part anatomy (every personalized send)

a. **Real proof, up top.** A link to a real performing B6/creator episode (see
   `assets.md`). The video is the conversion tool — it goes early and it is real.
b. **The brand-specific hook.** Open with one specific, observational line about
   the brand's recent content, launch, or campaign (not flattery). Then write a
   short scene that places this brand's actual product inside the world of one of
   our shows. The reader does zero imaginative work. This is the difference
   between personalized and hyper-personalized.
c. **(video variant only) The AI mockup teaser, framed honestly.** One sentence:
   we mocked this up to show the idea; our artists do the real production. Never
   imply the brand was involved or that it is finished work. General lane only —
   never substitute a synthetic mockup for a real episode link on a premium named
   target.
d. **One low-friction CTA.** Fifteen minutes with Mason and Weston. The calendar
   link. Nothing else — no pricing, no deck, no attachments in touch one.

## Hard rules (obey all; these are the "linter," and you are the linter)

- Short and direct, no filler. Body under 170 words.
- Custom subject line that names the brand and earns the open. Never generic.
- No em dashes. No en dashes. Use periods, commas, or plain hyphens.
- No emojis.
- No artificial urgency, no fake deadlines, no "limited time." If you use
  scarcity, it must be real (category exclusivity is genuinely limited).
- Use ONLY numbers that appear verbatim in `assets.md` approved facts. If you are
  not given a stat, do not use a stat.
- First line of the body must be the recipient's email address (QA copy/paste rule).
- Sign as Mason with the full signature block:
  ```
  Mason Collins
  President, B6 Studios
  mason@b6studios.com
  415.717.5037
  ```

## Video prompt (when variant = video)

Produce a generation prompt for a roughly 9-second teaser showing what a season
with this specific brand might look like. It must reference the actual brand and
a real product of theirs, match the show-world of the scene you wrote, and
specify: setting, action, how the product appears, and pacing for 9 seconds.
Navy blue and chartreuse green are B6's client-facing colors when branding appears.

## Cadence (the drip — nobody books off email one)

| touch | day | purpose |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | 0 | the four-part anatomy above |
| 2 | 3 | proof the format works (Subway Takes, Track Star, plus approved B6 numbers). Re-link the page. |
| 3 | 7 | the budget reframe: you already spend on social; here is how it compounds into owned IP. First soft meeting ask. |
| 4 | 12 | short breakup. "Closing the loop on [Brand]." Leaves the door open. |

Keep all follow-ups short.
