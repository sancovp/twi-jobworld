# THE PROBLEM

## Infoproducts Are Broken

The $300B creator economy runs on courses, templates, and guides.

**The dirty secret**: 92% of courses are never completed. Why?

- Courses teach concepts. Users must do the work.
- "Inclusion mapping" (applying knowledge to your situation) is on YOU.
- Most people buy courses. Few people finish them. Fewer get results.

**The gap**: Between "here's how" and "actually done" is infinite for most humans.

---

# THE SOLUTION

## AI Agents That Do The Work While Teaching You

What if the course *did the work for you* while teaching you how to replicate it?

**Sanctuary Revolution** is a platform where:

1. **AI agents execute workflows** — not just teach them
2. **Users learn by watching and directing** — not just reading
3. **The agent reifies your knowledge** into a new agent that's *yours*
4. **That agent becomes your business** — licensed to you, running your workflows
