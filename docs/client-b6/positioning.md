# B6 — locked positioning (instruction; do not reinvent this language)

B6 builds shows for brands. Instead of one-off social posts that disappear, we
create episodic series for TikTok, Reels, and Shorts. Content people actually
come back to watch. Brands stop interrupting audiences and start entertaining them.

**Two models:**

1. **Sponsor an existing creator series.** B6 works with creators who already
   have audience and engagement (Teddy Alvarez-Nissen / 7 Stories, Chewkz /
   Surviving the Cows). A brand associates itself with proven content.
2. **Co-develop a custom branded series.** The brand funds it, B6 produces it,
   the brand gets its own season. This is the bigger play and the main thing the
   general engine sells.

Lead with model 2; use model 1 as live proof.

**TAM logic (why brands say yes):** any consumer brand doing roughly $20M+ in
revenue can afford this and should be experimenting with episodic short form,
because that is where attention is moving.

**The pitch is not "buy an ad." It is "own a show."**
